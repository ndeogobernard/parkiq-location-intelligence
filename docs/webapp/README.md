# ParkIQ Columbus Parking Explorer

Part of **ParkIQ: A Parking Lot Site Selection Location Intelligence Analysis**.

An interactive map of parking demand, supply and shortage in Columbus by time of week, showing
where a new paid lot could compete. The web app is being built in ArcGIS Experience Builder from
public hosted layers made by ParkIQ; an offline version of the same content is generated by the
pipeline.

## Layers

| Layer | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Parking shortage by hex and time of week | stalls short (or spare) within a short walk, for weekday day and evening, weekend day and evening, and events |
| Parking demand by hex | modeled demand in stalls for each time of week |
| Paid-parking hot zones | clusters short of parking where paid parking is within an 8-minute walk |
| Free-parking shortage areas | short of parking where parking is free (context only) |
| Parking lots and garages | mapped lots and garages with stall counts |
| Metered streets | metered block faces with spaces and hourly rates |
| Zoning: is a paid parking lot allowed? | Columbus zoning districts by whether a paid lot is allowed |
| Downtown parking zones A and B | where new surface lots are not allowed (Zone A) or need design review (Zone B) |
| Frequent bus stops | stops with a bus every 15 minutes or better, 7 to 9 am |
| Submarkets | Downtown, Arena District, Short North, University District, Brewery District, Franklinton |

The public layers carry no parcel identifiers, owner names or candidate sites.

## Offline version

```powershell
parkiq dashboard --market markets\franklin_oh.yaml --run-id <run_id> --out dashboard.html
```

One self-contained HTML file (no web requests): the shortage map with a time-of-week selector,
the hot-zone list, shortfall by zone and parking demand by source. Code:
[`parkiq/dashboard.py`](../../parkiq/dashboard.py).
